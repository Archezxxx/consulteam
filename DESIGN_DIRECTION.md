# Consulteam redesign direction

## Audience and outcome

The page is for school students in Kyrgyzstan and their parents. Its first job is to get a student to start the 15-question RIASEC test; its second job is to turn the result into a conversation about a realistic study route abroad.

## Three directions considered

### Editorial map

A typographic, magazine-like page with one large route map and restrained colour. Strong for authority and detailed content, but too close to the current quiet editorial treatment.

Hero composition: large left-aligned claim with a map-like RIASEC diagram. Type: expressive grotesk plus neutral sans. Colour: stone, navy, one green accent. Signature interaction: a route line responds to the selected career type.

### Loud flat poster

A bold street-poster page with huge type, hard-cropped photographs and saturated blocks. Energetic for students, but the visual noise risks competing with the test itself.

Hero composition: full-width headline with a tilted photo. Type: ultra-heavy grotesk. Colour: coral, black and acid green. Signature interaction: photo shutter on load.

### Branded colour chapters chosen

Each section is a flat colour chapter, held together by one friendly geometric typeface and a fixed grid. The company cobalt, deep navy, white and lime build the main hierarchy; the six RIASEC colours appear only where they carry meaning.

Hero composition: a short two-line title beside a giant cropped RIASEC route shape. Type: Chillax 400, 500 and 600. Colour: company cobalt opener, deep navy, white, lime accent and pale blue. Signature interaction: the RIASEC shape floats gently; the real live interaction is the career test.

## Design thesis

Bright colour should clarify the student's route, not decorate empty space. Every screen carries one decision: understand yourself, see the six types, take the test, compare countries, prepare for an exam, or contact an adviser.

## Subject inventory

- Consulteam monogram and name
- 15 situational questions
- Six RIASEC career types
- A three-letter Holland code
- Kyrgyz, Russian and English languages
- Country photographs and detailed country presentations
- Scholarships, tuition and living-cost information
- IELTS, TOEFL and SAT preparation
- Baytik Tower in Bishkek
- WhatsApp consultation
- The sequence from career choice to university admission

## Argument by section

1. The student can identify a direction in seven minutes. Proof: the real 15-question test is on the page.
2. A useful result is a mix, not one label. Proof: six types and the three-letter Holland code.
3. A direction becomes a study route. Proof: countries, costs, scholarships and university presentations.
4. Admission requirements are concrete. Proof: IELTS, TOEFL and SAT target ranges.
5. A consultant can turn the result into next actions. Proof: a prefilled WhatsApp handoff.

## Idea and live thing

On this page, the student's three-letter Holland code becomes a sequence of bright colour chapters that leads from self-discovery to a country and an admission plan.

The live thing is the full 15-question RIASEC test. It works on a phone, is keyboard accessible and produces a ranked profile without sending answers to a server.

## Aircraft livery

The existing 3D aircraft keeps its detailed shape. Its livery uses a white fuselage, cobalt nose and vertical tail, navy underside and wordmark, and small lime wing tips. The Consulteam monogram appears in white on both sides of the tail. These large colour fields remain recognizable while the aircraft is small and moving around the globe.

## Layout system

- One 12-column grid with 16–28px equal outer margins
- Full-bleed colour fields with hard transitions
- One dominant element per screen
- Headings share one left anchor and remain two lines or fewer
- Photos remain square or 4:5 with no decorative frame
- No card grids, gradients, hairlines, resting shadows or eyebrow labels

## Type and colour tokens

- Display and text: Chillax
- Company cobalt: `#164fc9`
- Company deep navy: `#1f3265`
- Company lime: `#d8f55a`
- Ground: `#f3f6fb`
- Paper: `#ffffff`
- Ink: `#10141b`
- RIASEC semantic colours: blue, green, yellow, purple, coral and pale grey

## Mobile adaptation

The hero becomes type first and the route shape second. Colour chapters stay full width, growing tiles stack vertically, country tiles become a two-column then one-column gallery, and all controls stay at least 48px tall. Headline copy is shortened per language instead of being scaled down until it loses presence.

## Anti-patterns

- No muted editorial palette detached from the company identity
- No small uppercase labels or decorative numbering
- No thin rule dividers
- No rows of equal white cards
- No gradients or glass panels
- No shadows on resting elements
- No headline longer than two lines
